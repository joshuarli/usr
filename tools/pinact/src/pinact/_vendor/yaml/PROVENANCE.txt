Vendored files: every pure-Python module from the sdist's lib/yaml/
(__init__, composer, constructor, cyaml, dumper, emitter, error, events,
loader, nodes, parser, reader, representer, resolver, scanner, serializer,
tokens). The cyaml shim is kept for license completeness; it degrades to the
pure-Python loader because no yaml._yaml C extension is vendored
(yaml.__with_libyaml__ is False). No pip, uv, site-packages, or network
access is needed at runtime; the tool runs under ``python3 -I``.

Verified at vendoring time: yaml.compose(<doc with `on:` key>,
Loader=yaml.BaseLoader) keeps `on:` a string.
